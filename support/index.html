Support

Help for MAJRA for iPhone.

Contact us: email majra.support@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and your MAJRA version, which you can find in MAJRA › Settings › About MAJRA.

Free trial and MAJRA Lifetime

  • MAJRA starts with a free 14-day trial through the App Store.
  • After the trial, MAJRA Lifetime unlocks the app with a single purchase. There is no subscription. The App Store shows the price before you confirm.
  • When the trial ends, MAJRA shows a page to buy, redeem an offer code or restore until it is unlocked. Nothing is deleted: your data stays on your iPhone and is all there again once MAJRA is unlocked.
  • To use an offer code, open MAJRA › Settings › MAJRA Lifetime › Redeem Offer Code, or use the same option on the page shown when the trial has ended.

Restoring your purchase

  • Open MAJRA › Settings › MAJRA Lifetime › Restore purchases. Restore is also on the trial screen and on the page shown when the trial has ended.
  • Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If nothing is found, check that you are signed in to the App Store with that account, then try again.

Your data and a new iPhone

  • Your programs, records, weights and profile are stored only on your iPhone, in an encrypted database, so we cannot see or recover them.
  • To move to a new iPhone, restore it from an iCloud or computer backup of your old iPhone, with iCloud Keychain turned on. MAJRA’s encryption key arrives through iCloud Keychain. Without it, the data in the backup cannot be opened.
  • If MAJRA shows “Your data is locked” after a restore, check that you are signed in to iCloud with Keychain turned on, wait for it to finish syncing, then tap Try again.
  • Do not tap Start fresh on that screen unless you accept losing that data. It deletes it permanently.

Deleting your data

Open MAJRA › Settings › Privacy and data › Delete All MAJRA Data. This permanently deletes your programs, records, weights, step goal and profile, and cannot be undone. Your purchase and trial are not affected.

Steps and Apple Health

  • MAJRA only reads your step count and walking and running distance. It never writes to Health.
  • To allow or remove access, open iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › MAJRA.
  • To change your daily step goal, open the Steps tab and tap Goal.

Language

Open MAJRA › Settings › App › Language to choose the app language, and separately the language of exercise and muscle names.

Sharing a program

Open a program and tap the share button at the top of the screen to send it as a link or a file. The person you send it to needs MAJRA to import it.
