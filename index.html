Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Applies to MAJRA for iPhone, version 1.3.0 and later.

In short: MAJRA does not collect your data. What you enter stays on your iPhone, in an encrypted database. We have no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we cannot see your data.

1. Who we are

MAJRA is a workout and progress tracker made by PWRBEEZ (“we”, “us”). For questions about this policy, email majra.support@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone

MAJRA keeps the following only on your device:

  • Your training programs: days, exercises, planned values, backoff sets and your top records.
  • Your training history in the app, such as completed days.
  • The body weight entries you log.
  • Your daily step goal.
  • Your profile: the name, height and starting weight you enter, and your kg/lb choice.
  • App preferences, such as appearance and language.

Everything except app preferences is kept in a database encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). Its key is created on your iPhone and stored in the iOS Keychain. MAJRA never sends this data to us or to anyone else.

3. Apple Health

If you allow it, MAJRA reads two types of data from Apple Health: step count, and walking + running distance. It uses them only to show your daily steps, your progress toward your step goal and your last 7 days.

  • MAJRA only reads. It never writes, changes or deletes Health data.
  • Health data is read when MAJRA shows it and is not saved by MAJRA. Only your step goal is saved.
  • Health data is never sent to us or to anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by MAJRA.
  • You can change access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › MAJRA.

4. Purchases

The 14-day free trial and the lifetime purchase are App Store in-app purchases handled by Apple. Offer codes are redeemed on Apple’s own screen.

  • We never receive your payment details, your Apple Account or your email address.
  • MAJRA checks your trial or purchase with Apple’s StoreKit, on your iPhone.
  • So that the trial cannot be restarted, MAJRA saves a small trial record in the iOS Keychain: the trial start time and two timing values. If you use iCloud Keychain, a copy syncs there. It contains nothing else.

5. Time check with Apple

To keep the trial period accurate even if the device clock is changed, MAJRA sometimes reads the current time from Apple’s website (www.apple.com). The request carries no identifier, no cookies and no MAJRA data. As with any internet connection, Apple’s server sees your IP address and basic technical details, such as the app and iOS version. We receive nothing. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

6. Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can use Sign in with Apple to fill in your name. MAJRA asks Apple only for your name, never your email. It keeps your name and a private user identifier from Apple on your iPhone, so it can tell if you stop using Sign in with Apple. No account is created, and no part of MAJRA depends on it. You can unlink it in MAJRA’s settings, or stop it in your iPhone’s Apple Account settings. MAJRA then removes the link.

7. iCloud and backups

MAJRA has no backup service of its own. If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your MAJRA data is included in that backup, and the database stays encrypted. The database key and the trial record sync through iCloud Keychain, which Apple protects with end-to-end encryption. These services belong to your Apple Account, and we cannot access them. Without a backup and iCloud Keychain, your data cannot be recovered, by you or by us.

8. Third parties

MAJRA contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and we do not sell or share your data. The only outside services MAJRA uses are Apple’s: Apple Health, the App Store, Sign in with Apple, iCloud and the time check above. The open-source libraries built into MAJRA run on your iPhone and send nothing to us or to others.

9. What Apple may share with us

Apple processes data under its own privacy policy. Through App Store Connect, Apple gives developers sales and download reports, and aggregated statistics and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers). If you test MAJRA through TestFlight, Apple also shares the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send. We use this information only to understand downloads and fix problems.

10. Contacting us

If you email majra.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed for your request. Our email service is Gmail, provided by Google.

11. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it.

  • To delete everything at once, open MAJRA › Settings › Privacy and data › Delete All MAJRA Data. This permanently deletes the encrypted database: programs, records, weights, step goal and profile. Your purchase and trial are not affected. App preferences, such as appearance and language, stay until you delete the app.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. The database key and the trial record can remain in the iOS Keychain. They contain none of your programs, weights or profile details.
  • Copies inside existing iCloud or computer backups remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.
  • We do not hold your MAJRA data, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You control it on your device. For any request, contact us.

12. Children

MAJRA does not collect data from anyone, including children.

13. Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone’s security and by the encrypted database. Protect your iPhone with a passcode. No method of protection is perfect.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version of MAJRA starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
